Her Journey

A practice rooted in
presence & breath

Nishu Yadav's relationship with yoga began not in a studio, but in stillness — a quiet searching that eventually led her to the mat. What started as a personal practice to find calm in the chaos of daily life became a lifelong calling to guide others home to themselves.

After completing her 200-hour Yoga Alliance Teacher Training, Nishu deepened her study across Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin, and Pranayama traditions. She has trained thousands of students — from complete beginners finding their first breath to experienced practitioners refining their alignment.

Nishu founded Yogorise with a simple belief: that every body deserves a gentle entry point into yoga, and every mind deserves the quietude that consistent practice creates. Her teaching style is warm, unhurried, and deeply human.

Teaching Philosophy

What Nishu believes
yoga should feel like

🌱

Meet Yourself Where You Are

Every class begins without expectation. There is no correct version of a pose — only the one that belongs to your body today.

🧘

Breath Before Depth

The quality of your breath is more important than the depth of your stretch. Nishu teaches breath as the real teacher — the body simply follows.

✨

Consistency Over Intensity

Ten quiet minutes on the mat every morning will transform you more than one heroic session a week. Small, steady practice is Nishu's core belief.
